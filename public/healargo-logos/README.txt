Healargo logo files
===================

Palettes
  Sesame Gold     background #2C1A12   ring #D4A44A   leaf/text #F4E6C8
  Leaf on White   background #FFFFFF   ring #C4921A   leaf/text #2F5A32

For each palette
  healargo-<slug>.png / .svg                 with background
  healargo-<slug>-transparent.png / .svg     logo elements only

PNGs are 4000px wide, rasterized from the SVG.
SVGs are true vectors traced with Potrace from the original raster
artwork's ring and leaf+wordmark layer masks (not an original
designer vector file). Letter counters (holes in e, a, g, o) are
preserved with even-odd fill.
